Decentralising Visual Arts Symposium 21/09/2026

Assisted with the 2026 Cromer Artspace National Symposium. Responsibilities included putting together and designing the slideshow, arranging speakers' slides, and planning for round tables and discussion panels. I also organised the technical requirements, including projection displays, wireless remotes for speakers to access their slides, technical backups and contingency plans. Working to a tight schedule during the day, I also brought together photographs taken throughout the panels, round tables and lunch break to create a visual presentation for the conclusion of the symposium.

Volunteer Supervisor / Photographer for GiveVision at Bristol's Forwards Festival

Volunteered as a supervisor and photographer on the disability platform at Bristol's Forwards Festival, helping people with visual impairments use specialist headsets. I was also responsible for photographing the use of the headsets for social media and publicity.
