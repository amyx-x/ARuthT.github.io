Digital Nervous System

2026 Graduate Show | Touch Designer, Blender, LED panel, Printed Photography

///

This art interactive installation is a multimedia collective demonstrating related themes around surrealism and the unconscious mind. This is shown via dreams; representation and symbolism of specific objects, some of which will be displayed on LED light panels. Further aspects include data from the brain; a process meant to explore the conscious and unconscious interactivity between human and machine. -this can be seen via the muse 2 headset setup, of which viewers are encouraged to wear. As they dawn the headset, the video art on the JVC television will distort and become unfamiliar with the use of their own brain waves. The conscious interaction includes the option to press keyboard buttons which will change the presentation of the screen or the way that their brainwaves alter the video.
