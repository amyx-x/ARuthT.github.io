Digital Nervous System

2026 Graduate Show | TouchDesigner, Blender, LED panel, printed photography

///

This interactive multimedia installation explores themes relating to Surrealism and the unconscious mind. Dreams are used as a starting point, with the representation and symbolism of specific objects appearing across the work, including on LED light panels. Further elements incorporate brain data, exploring the conscious and unconscious interactions between human and machine. Viewers are encouraged to wear a Muse 2 headset. As they put on the headset, the video artwork displayed on the JVC television distorts and becomes unfamiliar, responding to their own brainwaves. Conscious interaction is also encouraged through keyboard controls, which allow viewers to alter the screen presentation and influence how their brainwaves affect the video.
