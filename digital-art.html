Digital Art

After achieving my Level 5 Diploma in Creative Computing, my artwork took a turn towards technology. Using these newly acquired skills, I have explored projects with visual programming software TouchDesigner, 3D sculpting software Blender, and HTML, CSS and JavaScript coding in Visual Studio Code. In addition, I have investigated the use of older technologies, such as JVC broadcast monitors, and alternative forms of digital and technological display. This body of work explores the connection between human and machine.

Beyond the Music Box

2026 | Wooden cube with headphones

An interactive piece using TouchDesigner to generate sound. When the viewer strikes the top face of the box, the sound changes pitch with each moment of contact. Clap your hands together and hear the sound pan between the headphones.

Solar

2025 | Banking Hall Project

Collaborated with peers to create a visual representation of solar energy use across a range of years. Used Visual Studio Code to create responsive visual elements informed by live and recorded datasets.

Eye Tracking, User Interaction

2025 | TouchDesigner, Blender, Digital Photography

A visual programming piece incorporating photographic elements. The artwork uses a Python script to gather eye movement data through a webcam. This data is then fed into the software, where it can alter and distort the artwork. The 3D eyeball in the centre mimics the viewer's eye movements. The audio in this piece is self-edited and includes a combination of old mechanical toy sounds, distorted using Premiere Pro. These sounds also trigger certain effects within the programme.

Collage of Art Created with Algorithms

2024 | P5 Algorithmic Art

During my time in the Creative Computing diploma year, I was introduced to many digital creative techniques, one of which was learning how to create algorithms for art. These are some of the pieces created while learning P5 coding.

- Changing Sudoku grid: each click generates a new playable layout.

- Treble clef grid: each click generates a new score and a new set of grid colours.

- A range of yarn grid algorithms.
